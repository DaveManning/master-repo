The Legacy Moment

McKinsey's research indicates that AI adoption is facing a "Legacy Moment." The bottleneck isn't technology—it's organizational readiness. Leaders are failing to capture ROI because they treat AI as a tech upgrade rather than a business overhaul.

According to McKinsey's Eric Kutcher, successful AI implementation is 80% business transformation (process, culture, skills) and only 20% technology. ODI focuses on the 80% that others miss.
